# Anderson-Goodman Statistical Inference

The landmark paper by Anderson and Goodman, "Statistical Inference about Markov Chains" (1957), is the definitive mathematical reference for the $G^2$ likelihood-ratio test.
### Key Features
- Establishes statistical testing for transition matrix homogeneity and stationarity.
- Defines likelihood-ratio criteria for testing the order of a Markov chain (e.g., first-order vs. zero-order or second-order).
- In `github.com/stephen-mcelhose/markov`, this statistical framework is implemented under `infer.MarkovOrderTest` to enable practitioners to verify whether empirical transition data meets the Markov property.

## Key Points

- Canonical mathematical reference supporting `github.com/stephen-mcelhose/markov` capabilities.
- Highly relevant for state reduction, matrix solvers, or statistical estimation routines in Go.
- Synthesized and cross-referenced with related pages in the wiki database.
